Policy ID: P01D
Category: Competitor-Related Churn
Churn Reason (exact dataset value): Competitor offered higher download speeds
Frequency: 100 cases (5.4% of all churned customers)
Priority Level: MEDIUM-HIGH
Related Policy: P01A, P01B, P01C (other Competitor-Related sub-reasons); P06 (Speed/Bandwidth Concerns) - the KEY DIFFERENCE is that this policy applies when the customer explicitly compared speeds to a NAMED competitor's offering, while P06 applies when the customer simply found the company's own speed tiers unaffordable, with no competitor comparison mentioned.

Use When (customer profile hints):
- Internet Type = DSL (most likely to be out-competed on speed by Fiber-based competitors)
- Internet Service = Yes
- Customer has explicitly mentioned a competitor's speed/plan during a support interaction (if logged)

Problem Summary: The customer left because a competitor offered explicitly faster internet download speeds than the company's current offering to them - typically a DSL customer being offered Fiber-equivalent speeds elsewhere. This is a direct competitive technology/infrastructure comparison, distinct from P06 where the customer simply found the company's own faster tiers too expensive.

Recommended Retention Actions:
1. If the customer is on DSL and Fiber Optic is available in their area, offer a free or discounted trial upgrade to Fiber Optic (e.g., illustrative: first 1-2 months discounted) to demonstrate the speed improvement before they commit to switching providers.
2. If Fiber is not available in the customer's area, be transparent about infrastructure limitations and offer the best available speed tier at a retention discount.
3. Proactively flag DSL customers in areas with known competitor Fiber rollout for early retention outreach.

Note: Specific discount percentages and timeframes in these actions are illustrative examples only, not figures derived from company data.

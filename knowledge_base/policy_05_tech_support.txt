Policy ID: P05
Category: Technical Support Expertise Issues
Churn Reason (exact dataset values): Poor expertise of online support, Poor expertise of phone support
Frequency: 43 cases (2.3% of all churned customers)
Priority Level: MEDIUM
Related Policy: P02 (Support/Service Attitude Issues) - the KEY DIFFERENCE is that P05 is about whether the issue was actually resolved (competence gap), while P02 is about how the customer was treated (behavioral/manner gap). Use P05 when the complaint centers on the problem staying unresolved; use P02 when it centers on rudeness or poor manner.

Use When (customer profile hints):
- Recent unresolved technical support ticket/interaction logged (if available)
- Internet Service = Yes (technical issue likely connectivity/equipment related)
- Customer contacted support more than once for the same underlying issue (if such data is tracked)

Problem Summary: These customers had a technical problem that support staff (either online chat/self-service or phone support) were unable to resolve competently. Unlike the "attitude" group, the issue here isn't rudeness - it's a lack of technical knowledge or problem-solving ability, leaving the customer's actual issue unresolved and their confidence in the company's competence shaken.

Recommended Retention Actions:
1. Escalate this customer's unresolved technical issue immediately to a Tier 2/senior technical specialist to ensure the original problem is actually fixed.
2. Follow up personally once the issue is resolved to confirm satisfaction and rebuild confidence in the company's technical capability.
3. Offer a service credit or discount acknowledging the extended time/frustration caused by the unresolved issue (e.g., illustrative: credit proportional to time unresolved).
4. Flag this as a training/knowledge-base gap for the support team - review whether the specific issue type is well-documented in internal support resources.
5. For online self-service failures specifically, review and improve the relevant help-center article or chatbot flow that failed to resolve the customer's issue.

Note: Specific discount amounts in these actions are illustrative examples only, not figures derived from company data.

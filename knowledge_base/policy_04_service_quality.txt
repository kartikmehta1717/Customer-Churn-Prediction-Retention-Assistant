Policy ID: P04
Category: Service Quality & Reliability
Churn Reason (exact dataset values): Product dissatisfaction, Network reliability, Service dissatisfaction, Limited range of services
Frequency: 249 cases (13.3% of all churned customers)
Priority Level: HIGH
Related Policy: P05 (Technical Support Expertise Issues) - overlaps when a network reliability issue was reported to support but not competently resolved; if the primary complaint is about support competence rather than the underlying network/product itself, prefer P05.

Use When (customer profile hints):
- Internet Type = Fiber Optic or DSL (network reliability sub-reason most relevant here)
- Multiple services subscribed (Streaming, Security, Backup, etc.) but customer still churned (suggests unmet needs despite feature availability, for "Limited range of services")
- No specific competitor named, no specific price complaint - this is about the product/service itself falling short of expectations

Problem Summary: This group of customers experienced genuine dissatisfaction with the core product or service itself - whether unreliable network connectivity, a product that didn't meet expectations, general service dissatisfaction, or a feeling that the company's service offerings were too limited compared to what they needed. This is fundamentally a product/infrastructure quality issue rather than a pricing or attitude issue.

Recommended Retention Actions:
1. For network reliability complaints, prioritize a technical diagnostic visit or remote line check to identify and resolve the specific connectivity issue in their area before they leave.
2. Offer a temporary service credit for the period during which service was unreliable (e.g., illustrative: prorated credit for downtime), as a tangible acknowledgment of the problem.
3. For customers citing "limited range of services," proactively present bundled service upgrades (e.g., adding streaming, security, or backup services) that may address the gap they were feeling.
4. Assign a dedicated account specialist to conduct a needs-assessment call, understanding specifically what the customer felt was missing or unreliable, and tailor a retention offer to that specific gap.
5. Where network infrastructure limitations are confirmed (e.g., area with poor coverage), be transparent with the customer about planned infrastructure improvements and timeline, and offer an interim discount.

Note: Specific discount amounts and timeframes in these actions are illustrative examples only, not figures derived from company data.

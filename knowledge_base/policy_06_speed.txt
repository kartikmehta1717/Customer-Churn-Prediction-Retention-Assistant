Policy ID: P06
Category: Speed/Bandwidth Concerns
Churn Reason (exact dataset value): Lack of affordable download/upload speed
Frequency: 30 cases (1.6% of all churned customers)
Priority Level: MEDIUM
Related Policy: P01D (Competitor offered higher download speeds) - the KEY DIFFERENCE is that P06 applies when the customer found the company's OWN higher-speed plans too expensive, with NO competitor comparison mentioned. P01D applies when the customer explicitly compared speeds to a NAMED competitor's offering. If a competitor is mentioned, prefer P01D.

Use When (customer profile hints):
- Internet Type = DSL (wants faster speed but finds upgrade cost prohibitive)
- Monthly Charge is at or below average (suggests customer is on a lower/cheaper tier and can't justify upgrading)
- No competitor explicitly named in the complaint

Problem Summary: These customers wanted faster internet speeds but felt the plans offering those speeds were not affordably priced for them. This is a middle-ground issue between pure price sensitivity and pure service quality - they aren't necessarily unhappy with current speeds being broken, but they feel priced out of the speed tier they actually want or need, independent of any competitor comparison.

Recommended Retention Actions:
1. Offer a limited-time promotional rate on a higher-speed plan (e.g., illustrative: discounted rate on Fiber Optic for the first few months) so the customer can experience the upgrade before committing to full price.
2. Bundle a speed upgrade with a small discount on another service (e.g., streaming add-on) to increase perceived value without a direct price cut.
3. Review whether a mid-tier speed plan exists between the customer's current plan and the premium tier - sometimes customers churn because only "too slow" and "too expensive" options are visible to them.
4. For customers with multiple connected devices/heavy usage, explain data/speed plans that specifically match household size, which may reveal a more suitable (and better-perceived-value) option than what they currently have.

Note: Specific discount amounts and timeframes in these actions are illustrative examples only, not figures derived from company data.

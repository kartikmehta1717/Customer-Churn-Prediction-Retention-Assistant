Policy ID: P01A
Category: Competitor-Related Churn
Churn Reason (exact dataset value): Competitor had better devices
Frequency: 313 cases (16.7% of all churned customers)
Priority Level: HIGH
Related Policy: P01B (Competitor made better offer) - overlaps when customer mentions both device and general offer; P01C, P01D also part of the same Competitor-Related group

Use When (customer profile hints):
- Contract = Month-to-Month (most common among this group)
- Customer recently asked about handset/device upgrade eligibility
- Tenure is mid-to-long (customer has been eligible for upgrade for a while)

Problem Summary: The customer left specifically because a rival provider offered better hardware/devices (e.g., a newer phone model, better bundled equipment) than what this company offers or subsidizes. This is a hardware/device-value issue, distinct from pricing or data allowance issues.

Recommended Retention Actions:
1. Offer a device upgrade or trade-in promotion (e.g., illustrative: discounted upgrade to a current-generation device) for customers nearing contract renewal.
2. Check whether the customer is eligible for an early upgrade and proactively inform them before they compare competitor offers.
3. If matching the competitor's exact device deal isn't feasible, offer an equivalent-value alternative (e.g., illustrative: bill credit toward a device purchase).

Note: Specific discount percentages and timeframes in these actions are illustrative examples only, not figures derived from company data, and should be set by the retention/marketing team based on actual cost margins.
